## M0 exit gate

The M0 gate is satisfied only when the exact PR head proves all of the following:

- A1 Runtime, A2 Dependency, A3 Deprecation, A4 Build & Packaging, A5 Protocol & Schema, A6 Host Compatibility, and A7 CI & Toolchain are represented in the M0 records.
- Every MOD-001 through MOD-016 row has owner, consumers, risk, operational decision, execution state, evidence, and rollback ownership.
- No item remains `PROPOSED`; each is `APPROVED`, `DEFERRED`, or `REJECTED`.
- MOD-016 and any future T4 finding are not executable by Technology Modernization.
- M0 changes remain limited to governance documentation/tests and do not change runtime, dependency, Host, protocol/schema semantics, product behavior, or public contracts.
- The focused governance test and canonical repository regression are GREEN on the same exact PR head.

Until those checks are green, M1 is blocked. After they are green, M1 remains **NOT STARTED** until the M0 gate is reviewed/accepted; the M0 commit itself does not perform M1 work.
